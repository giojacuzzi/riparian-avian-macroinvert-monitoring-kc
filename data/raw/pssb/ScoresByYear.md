`ScoresByYear.csv` contains the Puget Sound Stream Benthos (PSSB) stream health (including benthic index of biotic integrity) data, obtained from the [Puget Sound Stream Benthos database](https://pugetsoundstreambenthos.org/Biotic-Integrity-Scores.aspx). Data columns specify site id and local code, stream basin, stream subbasin, stream name, ownership project, location, and B-IBI score during each year of sampling.
