The `traits` directory contains functional trait data used in the analysis, obtained from AVONET ([Tobias et al., 2022](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ele.13898)) and EltonTraits ([Wilman et al., 2014](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1890/13-1917.1)). Avian species dietary and foraging guild associations are specifiecd in `species_diets.csv` and `species_guilds.csv`.
